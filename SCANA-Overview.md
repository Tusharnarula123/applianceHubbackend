# Scana.in — Product Overview
**After-Sales Intelligence Platform for Appliance Brands**
Version 1.0 · June 2025

---

## What Is Scana.in?

Scana.in is a white-label SaaS platform that turns a QR code on any appliance into a complete after-sales service experience. Customers scan the code — no app download required — and instantly access AI support, warranty registration, claim filing, repair booking, and spare parts ordering. Brands get a branded portal and a real-time operations dashboard.

---

## The Problem We Solve

Appliance brands lose customers after the sale. Support calls are expensive, warranty registrations are missed, claims take days to process, and brands have zero visibility into what's failing in the field. Scana.in replaces this with a connected, digital experience for both the brand and the customer.

---

## Core Modules

**1. QR Code & Product Identification**
Every appliance gets a unique QR code. Scanning it opens a personalized, mobile-optimized web page pre-loaded with that product's details, warranty status, and service history — without the customer needing to log in first.

**2. AI Support Chatbot**
Powered by RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) over the brand's own product manuals and FAQs. Handles Tier-1 and Tier-2 queries 24/7 in multiple languages. Smart escalation to human agents when needed. Every session is logged and rated.

**3. Warranty Registration & Management**
Customers register in under 60 seconds. The system validates purchase date, calculates expiry, and issues a branded digital warranty certificate. Brands configure warranty terms per product category. Expiry reminders sent automatically.

**4. Claims Management**
Structured end-to-end workflow: submission → review → approval → resolution. Auto-checks warranty status. Assigns claim to a brand agent. Triggers repair dispatch on approval. SLA timers escalate stale claims automatically.

**5. Repair Dispatch & Field Service**
Brand admins assign jobs to technicians; customers get real-time SMS updates. Technicians log work performed, parts used, and resolution notes via a mobile view. Post-service satisfaction survey auto-sent on completion.

**6. Spare Parts Catalog**
Brands publish a parts catalog filtered by model compatibility. Customers and technicians order genuine parts directly. Stock tracked in real time; low-stock alerts sent to admins.

**7. Analytics Dashboard**
Live KPIs: QR scans, active warranties, open claims, CSAT, avg resolution time. Charts for claim trends, top failure patterns, and technician performance. Exportable reports. Weekly digest email every Monday.

---

## Technical Foundation

Built on NestJS + TypeORM + MySQL 8 with a React/Next.js brand portal. Fully multi-tenant — each brand is an isolated tenant with its own subdomain, logo, and data. API-first with Swagger docs, JWT auth, and role-based access (Owner / Admin / Manager / Agent). Mobile-first consumer experience works on any smartphone browser.
