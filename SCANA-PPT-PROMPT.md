### DESIGN RULES (apply to every slide)

- Every slide must have exactly ONE headline that is the takeaway — if someone reads nothing else, this line alone should land.
- No slide should have more than 40 words of running text total (not counting labels, stats, or captions).
- Use icons on every slide — real icons, not placeholder squares.
- Stats should be HUGE — 48pt minimum for key numbers.
- Dark slides: use teal for accents, gray for secondary text, white for primary text.
- Light slides: use navy for headings, teal for accents, dark gray for body.
- Every data point gets a small source citation at the bottom of the slide.
- Avoid: stock images of people shaking hands, generic globe icons, clipart, default templates.
- Include: product screenshots/mockups, real numbers, clean charts, minimal but intentional visuals.
